Represent Decimals

5th Grade Math · Page 2 (Set 13)

I. Introduction

There are three ways to represent a decimal number.

Example: the number 5.1711

  • Standard form — the whole decimal number: 5.1711
  • Expanded form — split by place value:
    5.1711 = 5 + 110 + 7100 + 11000 + 110000
  • Word form — write it in words:
    “Five and one thousand seven hundred eleven ten-thousandths”
Place-value reminder: .1 = tenths · .01 = hundredths · .001 = thousandths · .0001 = ten-thousandths
