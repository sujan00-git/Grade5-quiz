Problem Solving

5th Grade Math · Page 3 (Set 13)

I. Multiplication and Divisions

Remember the order of operations: do × and ÷ first (left to right), then + and −.

II. Round to the Nearest Hundred

Look at the tens digit: if it's 5 or more, round up; if less than 5, round down.

III. Fill in the Blanks — Expanded Form

Split each decimal into its place-value parts. Fill the digit for each place.

IV. Partition the Number

Break each decimal into how many hundreds, tens, units, tenths, hundredths, and thousandths it has.

V. Perimeter of a Rectangle

Perimeter = 2 × (length + width). Give the answer in cm.

VI. Subtract Fractions

Find a common denominator, then subtract the numerators. Write your answer as a fraction like 1/10.

VII. Math Trivia

A mix of shapes, measurement tools, and real-world word problems.
